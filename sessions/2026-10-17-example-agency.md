## Pitch

I run Northfield Creative, a 22-person marketing agency in Denver. Our ops coordinator left on September 30. Most of her month went into client reports for 31 retainer clients, onboarding, invoice reminders and keeping Asana tidy. Rehiring costs about $73,700 a year fully loaded and took 9 weeks last time. A consultant quoted $12,500 to set up Claude agents that draft the reports, run onboarding and chase invoices, with an account manager approving everything, plus $1,430 a month to run it. I lean towards the agents and skipping the hire. What I'm not sure about: a third of her hours were people and office work no agent will do, and two reports already went out late this month. Do we replace the role with agents, rehire, or test first?
