## Company

- Marketing agency in Denver, Colorado. 22 employees (W-2), owner-led: Dana, founder and CEO.
- 31 retainer clients and some project work. Tools: HubSpot, Asana, Google Workspace, Looker Studio, QuickBooks.
- Six people use ChatGPT or Claude on personal accounts. There is no company AI policy yet.

## The AI option

Source: written quote from an outside consultant, October 12.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Setup, 6 weeks, fixed price | $12,500 |
| Claude Team seats for 22 people and API usage | about $780 a month |
| Consultant care plan (12-month minimum) | $650 a month |
| **Year one** | **$29,660** |
| **Following years** | **$17,160** |

- Scope: agents draft the monthly reports from the exports (the account manager approves every report), run the
  onboarding checklist and access requests, draft invoice reminders for approval, write the weekly Asana digest.
  A shared knowledge base with SOPs and client facts is part of the setup.
- One account manager, Priya, would own the agents: **assumption** 6 extra hours a month for approvals and fixes.
  Her fully loaded cost is about $55 an hour.
- Not covered by the agents: vendors and office (21 h) and people stuff (30 h), together **51 hours a month**.
  Who takes them is **open**.

## Clients and contracts

- 9 of the 31 retainer contracts (MSAs) say client data may only be processed by "approved subcontractors".
  No client has been asked yet.
- Retainer churn last year: 4 of 31 clients. Reasons given: price (2), results (1), "felt like a number" (1).
