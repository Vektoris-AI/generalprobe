## Who
2–4 sentences: role, experience, why this person sits on the council.

## What she/he watches
The criteria this member judges an idea by. Concrete, with numbers where they exist.

## Questions
5–8 questions, phrased the way this member would ask them.

## Red flags
What reliably leads to a No.

## Thinking patterns
3–6 rules of thumb, methods, or experiences the judgment comes from (with a source, if you have one).

## Tone
How formal, sentence length, temperament, typical short phrases.

## Vote
When does this member say Yes, when "Yes, if …", when No, and which conditions do they typically set?

## How I decide
Four criteria with anchors (0–3 / 4–6 / 7–8 / 9–10), plus K.O. criteria and blind spots. The math and the decision rule are in `skill/urteilsregeln.en.md`.
- **P1 Lead question** (counts double): how you recognize 0–3, 4–6, 7–8 and 9–10.
- **P2** a second criterion from "What she/he watches", with its own anchors.
- **P3 Price and value:** according to the shared rules.
- **P4 Plan for my area:** Is there a concrete, checkable plan for the risk in this member's area?

**K.O.:** one or two points from the red flags that reliably lead to No.

**Where my view is off:** When this member is systematically too strict or too lenient.
